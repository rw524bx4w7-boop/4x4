## ⚠️ **RISKS AND MITIGATION**

### **Primary Risks**

#### **1. Subscription Price Resistance**
- **Risk:** Companies prefer pure per-contract model
- **Mitigation:** Flexible monthly terms vs annual commitments
- **Alternative:** Offer both models, let market decide

#### **2. Tier Misalignment**
- **Risk:** Companies don't fit neatly into tier structure
- **Mitigation:** Custom enterprise pricing for large accounts
- **Solution:** Flexible geographic and volume combinations

#### **3. Revenue Cannibalization**
- **Risk:** Lower per-contract fees reduce total revenue
- **Mitigation:** Subscription base more than compensates
- **Validation:** Net revenue increase of 30% in projections

### **Mitigation Strategies**

#### **Flexible Pricing Options**
- **Month-to-month:** No long-term commitment required
- **Custom tiers:** Tailored pricing for unique situations
- **Hybrid alternatives:** Pure subscription or pure per-contract options

#### **Performance Guarantees**
- **Lead quality standards:** Minimum conversion rate guarantees
- **Service level agreements:** Response time and uptime commitments
- **Satisfaction guarantee:** 30-day money-back policy
