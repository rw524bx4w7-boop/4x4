## 💡 **SLIDE 3: INTERPOLATION QUOTES STRATEGY**

### **The Technical Approach**

#### **Phase 1: Consumer Browsing (Free)**
- **Display Method:** Interpolated quotes from historical data
- **Cost to B4uSign:** $0 per quote view
- **User Experience:** Instant quotes, multiple provider options
- **Purpose:** Lead qualification and initial interest

#### **Phase 2: Purchase Intent (Paid)**
- **Trigger:** Customer clicks "Get This Plan" or "Buy Now"
- **API Call:** Real-time PEN integration for actual pricing
- **Cost to B4uSign:** $12 per API call
- **Revenue:** $15 pass-through fee to warranty company

### **Cost Efficiency**
```
Traditional Model: $12 per quote view
Interpolation Model: $12 only at purchase
Cost Reduction: 97% (only 3% of viewers purchase)
```

#### **2. Consumer Browses Options**
- **Interactive Comparison:** Coverage levels, deductibles, terms

- **Final Quote:** Binding quote with e-signature capability
- **Revenue Capture:** $350 average per completed contract

## ⚠️ **SLIDE 10: RISK MITIGATION**

### **Primary Risks & Solutions**

#### **1. Interpolation Accuracy Risk**
- **Risk:** Quotes significantly different from real-time pricing
- **Mitigation:** Continuous calibration with actual API data
- **Target:** <5% variance between interpolated and real quotes

#### **2. Subscription Adoption Risk**
- **Risk:** Companies prefer pure per-contract model
- **Mitigation:** Offer both options, flexible month-to-month terms
- **Strategy:** Demonstrate ROI through pilot programs

#### **3. Competitive Response Risk**
- **Risk:** Established players copy our model
- **Mitigation:** Network effects and superior technology
- **Defense:** Exclusive partnerships and first-mover advantages

### **Risk Monitoring**
